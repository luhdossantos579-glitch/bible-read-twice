About The Bible, Read Twice

The Bible, Read Twice reads one verse closely, follows one thread through history and archaeology, and reads it again. Sourced, respectful.

Who makes this

The Bible, Read Twice is published on YouTube by Vision Media. We do our own research and write our own scripts. The reading voice is text-to-speech, and each video description says so plainly.

How we work

What we say about a word, a place or a find comes from a scholar, translation or excavation report we name, and we don't rule between traditions. The sources for each video are in its description, and for articles at the foot of the page. Images are public domain, openly licensed or our own, listed per video on the credits page.
